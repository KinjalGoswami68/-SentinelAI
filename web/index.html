── 02 ── how it works ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Math to notice. One LLM call to explain.

01 · detect

Compare with normal

Each answer is embedded and compared with the bot's own normal answers. Plain math (nearest neighbours and CUSUM), no LLM, no cost per answer.

02 · investigate

Gather evidence, then write

Code compares the numbers, clusters the changed answers and lines up version changes. Then one LLM call writes the note, and every claim must cite those results by id.

03 · alert

One incident, not fifty pings

Signals become one incident, with at most 3 Slack messages: opened with the note, escalated, resolved.

opened ──▶ escalated
   └──────▶ resolved
      max 3 messages

Built to catch when your bot's behaviour changes, not to fact-check single answers. Limits

── 04 ── install ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Three lines.

Get a key in the app, then post each answer after your bot replies:

requests.post("%API_BASE%/ingest", headers={"X-API-Key": KEY},
              json={"prompt": question, "response": answer})
